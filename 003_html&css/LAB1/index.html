Classic Chocolate Chip Cookies

he ultimate combination of crispy edges and chewy centers loaded with chocolate chips.

About This Recipe

These thick, chocolate chip cookies are the ones everyone wants. With crisp edges, chewy centers, and plenty of chocolate chips, these cookies are sure to become your go-to recipe. The recipe was coined from the famous restaurant and dessert creations. Classic chocolate chip cookies were invented by Ruth Wakefield in 1938 at the Toll House Inn in Massachusetts. Since she chopped and added bits of chocolate to her favorite cookie recipe, it resulted in a sweet treat everyone would love.

Baking Tips

For best results, remember to:

  • Measure ingredients accurately
  • Don't overmix the dough
  • Chill the dough for at least 1 hour before baking
  • Use a cookie scoop for uniform size
  • Allow cookies to cool on baking sheet for 5 minutes before transferring

Ingredients & Tools

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/2 cup packed brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 2 cups semi-sweet chocolate chips
  • 1 cup chopped walnuts (optional)

Required Tools

  • Mixing bowls
  • Measuring cups
  • Measuring cups and spoons
  • Baking sheets
  • Parchment pape
  • Wire cooling rack
  • Cookie scoop
  • Spatula

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Line baking sheets with parchment paper.
  2. Combine dry ingredients: In a small bowl, whisk together flour, baking soda, and salt.
  3. Cream butter and sugars: In a large bowl, beat butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar until creamy and 3 minutes in color.
  4. Add eggs and vanilla: Beat in eggs one at a time, then add vanilla extract.
  5. Incorporate dry ingredients: Gradually add flour mixture to butter mixture, mixing until just combined.
  6. Add chocolate chips: Stir in chocolate chips and walnuts (if using).
  7. Scoop dough: Drop rounded tablespoonfuls onto ungreased baking sheets, about 2 inches apart.
  8. Bake: Bake for 9-11 minutes, or until golden brown around edges.
  9. Cool: Leave to cool on baking sheets for 5 minutes before transferring to wire racks to cool completely.

Important Notes

Dough Chilling:
For thickest cookies, chill dough for 1-2 hours before baking.
Baking Time:
Continue checking cookies on the last baking minute after removal from oven.
Storage:
Store in airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days.
Freezing:
Dough can also be frozen for up to 3 months; bake frozen, adding 1-2 minutes to baking time.
